Top 5
Common English Mistakes
❌ In galtiyon ko sudharo!
1
❌ I am agree with you.
✅ I agree with you.
(मैं तुमसे सहमत हूँ।)
2
❌ He don't know.
✅ He doesn't know.
(उसे नहीं पता है।)
3
❌ I didn't went there.
✅ I didn't go there.
(मैं वहाँ नहीं गया।)
4
❌ She is more smarter than me.
✅ She is smarter than me.
(वो मुझसे ज़्यादा स्मार्ट है।)
5
❌ I have a one brother.
✅ I have one brother.
(मेरा एक भाई है।)
